# Manrope

Troit embeds Manrope Regular (400), Medium (500), SemiBold (600), Bold (700), and ExtraBold (800) on both Android and Windows. All Material typography roles use the bundled family. Unsupported writing systems use the platform fallback fonts.

Source: the [Google Fonts Manrope distribution](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope), retrieved 2026-11-11. Static instances were generated from its `Manrope[wght].ttf` with FontTools at the corresponding weight coordinates. The source variable font is retained locally under `build/font-sources/`.

The SIL Open Font License and copyright notice are packaged in both apps as `raw/manrope_ofl.txt`.
